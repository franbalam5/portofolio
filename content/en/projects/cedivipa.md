# CEDIVIPA: Comprehensive Clinic Management System

A platform built to streamline the daily operations of a dental clinic, replacing paper archives and scattered spreadsheets with a centralized database and role-based access profiles.

![CEDIVIPA dashboard](/images/projects/cedivipa-dashboard.png)
*Main view of the clinical control panel and schedule.*

---

### Core features

- **Digital clinical records:** Detailed logging of diagnoses, medical history, and treatment progress per patient.
- **Document generation:** Template engine that exports clinical reports and prescriptions to standardized PDF format for printing or sending.
- **Appointments & ticketing:** Clear view of available slots, visit statuses, and reminders.
- **Access control:** Strict privilege separation between administrative staff, dentists, and assistants.

### Stack

- **Backend:** Native / modular PHP, prioritizing fast response times and low server load.
- **Database:** MariaDB with a normalized relational schema to ensure consistency in clinical histories.
- **Frontend:** HTML5, vanilla JavaScript, and Bootstrap with custom styles.
