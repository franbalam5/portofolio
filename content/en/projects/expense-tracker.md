# Smart Expense Tracker & Notification Parser

A solution built out of personal necessity: strict tracking of daily finances without depending on third-party banking services or cloud platforms charging monthly fees or leaking data.

![App screenshot](/images/projects/expense-tracker-mockup.png)
*UI built entirely with Jetpack Compose.*

---

### How it works

1. **Local event capture:** Uses Android's native `NotificationListenerService` to intercept alerts generated by authorized banking apps.
2. **Regex parsing engine:** Analyzes message content (amount, merchant, date, charge type) on-device.
3. **Decentralized storage & sync:** Stores structured records in flat formats (JSON / CSV) on the phone's filesystem. Via **Syncthing**, these files sync peer-to-peer and encrypted to my workstation for analysis in local scripts.

### Technical highlights

- **Mobile frontend:** Designed with **Jetpack Compose**, focused on clean transaction views and fast manual categorization when a format doesn't match.
- **Zero-Cloud approach:** No external telemetry. Data never leaves the local network.
- **Battery efficiency:** Background service with low resource consumption and strict exclusion rules for non-financial notifications.
