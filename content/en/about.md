# A bit about me

I go by **franbalam** online. I'm a software developer drawn to the intersection of simplicity and technical efficiency. I'd rather use reliable, transparent tools than frameworks that pile on unnecessary layers of complexity.

### My environment and preferences
- **Operating system:** Linux daily driver — Arch Linux, minimal tiling window managers.
- **Editor & terminal:** VSC for big projects, lightweight terminals for everything else, and keyboard shortcuts so my hands stay on the keys.
- **Data philosophy:** I believe in privacy, human-readable plain-text formats, and local-first solutions.
