This post marks the beginning of this page, a project that has been living in my mind for quite a few years, that I just couldn't get myself to do due to indecision. 

## Why Hugo

The short answer: I saw it used somewhere else and liked it.

The longer answer is that I've worked on many projects using different approaches to web development. I became familiar with tools such as React and Tailwind, but I realized I wasn't learning anything new by using the same tools repeatedly, so I decided to try something different for this project. It also turns out that tools that update frequently often break things just as often.

## Why the indecision

I've never liked having parts of my life encapsulated in one place, hence my lack of social accounts — this will probably be the only place where I share anything for a long time. My opinions shift, life goes on, and, most importantly, I dislike feeling like I don't own my work. I don't own Hugo, nor this domain in its entirety, but by doing this I get the feeling that I'm building something for myself, which makes this place feel like mine. How do you know when you own your work or your ideas? I still don't know the answer, but sometimes things just feel right when I do projects like this, which is what led me to programming in the first place.

## On building this site

Going back to my previous questions, I didn't know where to start, which made me overthink and try to devise a perfect scheme that would be completed in a single master stroke. But things don't work that way in real life.

> No plan survives first contact.

So I stopped thinking and started working on it.

Since the Internet already hosts so many similar sites, I wondered: how do I make this one my own? It turned out to be simpler than I expected. You choose colors and fonts that make you think "this looks pretty good", then you experiment, iterate, and eventually push your work for others to see, and then end up returning to step one.

Here you'll find content that's not only programming-related. I also enjoy literature and poetry, so you'll see sprinkles of those on the site.
